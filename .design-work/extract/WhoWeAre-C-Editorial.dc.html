Profile — Who we are

Two developers, one city, one standard.

"We're two developers from Guatemala City who wanted to build the technology we couldn't find anywhere else."

Connecta started in Guatemala City, where we both grew up. We're developers first — and everything we build rests on the same two convictions: great design isn't optional, and the technology behind it should be genuinely state of the art, not whatever's safest to ship.

We started this company to create tech experiences that don't feel like anyone else's — starting with our own product.

Founded by two developers, both raised in Guatemala City.
