Interaction concept

The diagram stays in view; focus moves as you scroll

The system map from Variant B is pinned in the viewport while the section scrolls. As each capability's row passes, its node brightens and a short expanded note appears beside it. Every name and one-line description stays visible at all times — the animation only adds detail on top, it never hides the baseline.

Why this avoids the "confusing for a lead" risk: the labels and descriptions in the diagram itself are never hidden behind the animation — a visitor who doesn't scroll slowly, or who has motion reduced, still reads all four names and lines at a glance (frame 0). The expanded note is a bonus for whoever lingers, not a gate. Recommend keeping the whole thing short (4 short scroll-triggers, no pinning beyond the diagram's own height) so it never traps the scroll the way a long "scrollytelling" section can.
